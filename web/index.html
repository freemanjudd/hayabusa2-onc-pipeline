About these images

These are real ONC-W2 frames captured for optical navigation during Hayabusa2's Earth flyby, not for scientific imaging. Two things follow from that:

  • Readout smear. ONC-W2 is a shutterless frame-transfer CCD: while the image is clocked off the sensor, bright Earth keeps exposing the pixels in its column, leaving a vertical band. USGS ISIS hyb2onccal does not correct this, so the pipeline removes it with a per-column background subtraction estimated from the (always-sky) top and bottom edge rows.
  • Exposure. The short nav exposures (~4 ms) drive Earth close to saturation. Frames are shown with an asinh stretch so the disk and terminator stay legible instead of clipping to white.
